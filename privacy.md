# The Eras Roleplay: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. Operated by Eras Development.

This explains what the Eras Bot and our priority shop collect and why.

## What we store

For priority purchases we store your Discord user ID, your priority tier, when it expires, and your number of skip passes. The server also sees your Discord ID when you connect so it can apply your priority.

The bot also handles whitelist applications and support tickets, which store the answers and messages you submit.

## Payments

Payments are handled by Discord or Stripe. We never see or store your card number or bank details. We receive only confirmation that a purchase was made.

## How we use it

Only to run the login queue, grant what you bought, handle support, and keep the server safe. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers or data brokers.

## Retention and deletion

We keep purchase records while your account is in use and as long as needed for refunds and disputes. To have your records deleted, open a support ticket in our Discord server.

## Contact

Open a support ticket in The Eras Roleplay Discord server.
