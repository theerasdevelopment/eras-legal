# The Eras Roleplay: Terms of Service

Last updated: October 2026. Operated by Eras Development.

These terms cover the Eras Bot on our Discord server and the optional priority purchases described below. By buying anything you agree to them.

## What you can buy

**Skip passes** are one-time items. Using one moves you to the front of the login queue for one connection. A pass is used up when you board the server.

**Monthly priority tiers** (Business and First Class) place you ahead of players without priority while the subscription is active. Higher First Class tiers board ahead of Business tiers.

Priority only affects waiting time to join the server. It gives no in-game money, items, advantages, or whitelist access. You still need to be whitelisted to play.

## Your account

Purchases attach to the Discord account used to buy. To get priority you must connect to the server with that Discord account open.

## Subscriptions and refunds

Monthly tiers renew until cancelled. If you cancel, priority continues until the end of the period you paid for. Used skip passes are not refundable. Unused passes and unused subscription time may be refunded at staff discretion within 14 days of purchase, or where the law requires.

## Rules and removal

Priority does not exempt you from server rules. Staff may remove priority and passes from accounts that are banned from the server or that break the rules, and may refund the remainder at their discretion.

## Availability

The server is a hobby project provided as is. We do not guarantee uptime, a particular wait time, or that priority will always let you in instantly when the server is full. We may change prices or features, and changes never affect a period you have already paid for.
